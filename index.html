Important Disclaimer

This platform is an academic research prototype developed by MBA students as part of a capstone project.

All data displayed on this platform — including permit status, rebuild intentions, building materials, contractor information, investor activity, and neighborhood momentum indicators — is either simulated, anonymized, or used for demonstration purposes only.

This tool does not reflect actual recovery efforts, real permit filings, actual resident intentions, or verified property information in Pacific Palisades or any other community.

This platform should not be used to make financial, legal, insurance, construction, or real estate decisions of any kind.

The research team makes no representations about the accuracy, completeness, or timeliness of any information displayed.

Data sources used in this prototype include publicly available LA County parcel boundaries, LA County fire damage assessment data (DINS), and publicly available school location data. These sources are used for academic demonstration purposes only.

Any resemblance to specific individuals, properties, or actual recovery outcomes is coincidental.
